# Pulse Emergency Hospital: Mobile Device and Remote Access Policy

Demo policy of a fictional organization (Vector demo tenant). Not legal advice.

## Scope
All laptops, phones and tablets that access patient health records, including personally owned devices, at Pulse Emergency Hospital, Mumbai.

## Requirements
- Devices must use full-disk encryption and a screen lock of 5 minutes or less.
- Remote access to the HIS and EMR requires multi-factor authentication and the hospital VPN.
- Patient data must not be stored in personal cloud storage, messaging apps such as personal WhatsApp groups, or public AI chatbots.
- Lost or stolen devices are reported to the IT helpdesk within 1 hour so they can be wiped remotely. If patient data may be exposed, the Breach and Security Incident Response Procedure applies (CERT-In within 6 hours; DPDP Act intimation).
- IT Security reviews mobile device access logs every quarter and keeps them for at least 180 days in India.

## Why
These safeguards are part of the reasonable security safeguards the hospital must maintain under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the IT Act, 2000.
