Your personal AI

Two kinds of AI. Both yours.

Every organization gets an internal AI that knows its work, running on physical AI it controls: a machine inside its own building.

Internal AI

Your own model, built on open weights and shaped by your documents, your approved answers and your rules.

  • Answers from your policies, with citations
  • Fine-tuned on approved examples from your work
  • Evaluated on your own test questions before it goes live
  • Applications beyond chat: drafting, redaction, review, triage

Physical AI

The hardware your AI runs on, inside your walls: a laptop for a demo, a server room for production, GPUs when you need them.

  • Ollama runtime on site, works fully air-gapped
  • Gemma by default, with the choice to add more models
  • Models, data, prompts and logs never leave the machine
  • Every action written to a tamper-evident audit chain

Select documents

Compliance and reports

Built-in compliance

Every rule that applies to you, layer by layer, and what it costs to ignore it.

Choose your service area and where you operate. Vector already knows the rules: international standards, national law, state or provincial law, local and board rules, and your own policies. It checks every document against them, shows what is done and what is pending, and estimates the probable loss if a rule is not complied with.

01Rules predefinedEverything your area and places require, already loaded.
02Run the checkOne button reads every uploaded document and every value entered. Checks differ by industry.
03Done and pendingPer rule: what is met, what is missing, what has expired.
04Assign what is neededDocuments, numbers and dates become tasks for the right team.
05Provided, re-checked, hashedEach input updates compliance at once and lands in the audit chain.

Probable loss is an illustrative model (exposure estimate × likelihood by status) built from statutory figures, meant to be replaced with your own estimates. Rule lists are a starting set to confirm with counsel; you add your own. Examples in small text show how a layer looks in one place; the same structure applies wherever you operate. Each industry has its own checks, and results depend on the documents each organization uploads.

The platform

Six things every deployment gets.

01

Private by architecture

Models, documents, prompts and answers stay on your hardware. Air-gapped installs are supported.

02

Owned intelligence

Evaluations, training data and model versions are yours, built from your work and portable.

03

Model freedom

Gemma today, any open-weight model tomorrow. Change models without changing your workflows.

04

Compliance built in

Obligations by industry and place, evidence, tasks, probable loss and printable reports.

05

Tamper-evident audit

Every action is a block in a SHA-256 hash chain, written in plain sentences: who did what, when.

06

Access that follows roles

Departments, roles and permissions decide who sees which documents and which AI answers.
